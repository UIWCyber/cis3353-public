3. Project Definition

Pick a scenario, then state the mission in one paragraph. Your capstone must Build the fictional environment, Attack it to demonstrate the threat before defenses, and Defend it by implementing and verifying controls.

4. Course Modules Integrated

Integrate at least three course modules (Ciampa 8th Edition, SY0-701). Modules 1 to 10 are lectured; modules 11 to 15 are allowed. For each, state the module and how it connects to your project.

5. System Architecture Plan

Describe the environment you will build: VMs, networks, firewalls, and key services.

6. Threat Demonstration Plan

Explain how you will show the attack working before defenses are in place. The attack must be demonstrable, with evidence captured.

7. Defense and Verification Plan

Name the controls you will implement and how you will prove their effectiveness after the attack.

8. Resources Required

List what you need to build and run the environment.
